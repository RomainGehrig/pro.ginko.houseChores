# Experience redesign — findings

Four analysts read the whole codebase and drove the running app on 2026-08-07: one mapping
the user journey and information architecture, one doing an interaction-heuristics pass, one
on mobile ergonomics and accessibility, one on habit and motivation design.

Design spec: `2026-08-07-experience-redesign-design.md`
Staged plan: `../plans/2026-08-07-experience-redesign.md`
Tracking issue: `hc-e92`

Findings cite files and line numbers against the tree at commit `b4586d7`. A spot-check of the
load-bearing claims was re-verified before this document was committed: the five native
dialogs, the absence of any `freezr.delete` call, `taskUpdateForOutcome` silently archiving
completed one-offs, the `|| 1` duration floor in `doingView.js:79`, and the 880 lines of
category/location tests all hold.

## Lens: journey
Headline: Chore Planner is organised around its data model rather than the four moments Romain actually opens it for, so the daily surface (Active Tasks) starts 1,542px down a 3,996px scroll, 11 of 17 tasks are silently overdue with no visual difference from one due in 2027, and a single page refresh mid-chore destroys the session.

Findings:
- [blocker] (global) There is no routing and no reload survival anywhere. index.js:25 unconditionally calls showView('tasks') on boot; state.js is a 4-line in-memory object holding currentSession/currentBundle/currentBundleIndex; a grep for pushState, hashchange, location.hash, localStorage and sessionStorage returns nothing. Nothing in the app ever queries sessions for status:'active' — listAllSessions is used only by historyView. The live DB already contains an orphaned {status:'active', timeBudgetMinutes:30, taskBundle:[2 ids], endTime:null} session. -> A refresh, a phone lock or a stray back gesture mid-chore erases the bundle, the elapsed timer and your position, drops you back on Tasks, and leaves a permanent 'abandoned' row in History that can never be resumed or removed.
- [blocker] (global) The nav is never guarded, so two sessions can run at once. All five .nav-btn elements report disabled:false at every moment, and the handler in index.js:10-15 is unconditional. Clicking 'Tasks' during Doing does not clear timerInterval (cleared only in renderCurrentTask/finishTask/endSession), leaves the session status:'active' in the DB, and sessionView.handleStart will overwrite state.currentSession and state.currentBundle wholesale. Symmetrically, leaving Review without pressing Finish never runs setNavVisible('review', false), so a stale Review button lingers pointing at an in-memory executionsCache from a session that ended. -> One mis-tap silently abandons a live session and orphans its record, and the app offers no warning, no resume, and no way to tell that the timer is still running behind a hidden view.
- [blocker] (tasks) 'Tasks' is four different screens stacked into one 3,996px scroll: a reference-data manager (#referenceManager, y=112), a capture box (.add-task-box, y=177), a triage queue (#proposedCards, y=404), the actual library (#activeCards, y=1542) and a graveyard (#archivedCards, y=3662). Measured live on an 844px viewport with only 17 active tasks and 2 proposed. -> The one surface you need every day starts almost two full viewports down, underneath a capture box you use weekly and a triage queue that is empty most of the time.
- [blocker] (tasks) Approval hard-requires a manually picked date, and the AI is explicitly forbidden from proposing one. buildNewTaskRecord sets scheduledDate:null; handleProposedClick calls readScheduleEditor(card, {requirePatternMatch:true}) which routes to validateScheduleInput (scheduleLogic.js:206-208) and fails with 'Enter a valid scheduled date.' on empty; buildEnrichmentPrompt (aiEnrich.js:12) instructs 'Do not suggest a scheduledDate; the user chooses it.' Meanwhile normalizeTaskSchedule (scheduleLogic.js:243-246) silently invents scheduledDate = today for any active task that lacks one. -> Every single task in a brain dump is blocked behind a native date picker the AI has been told not to help with, while the app happily fabricates dates without asking everywhere else.
- [blocker] (review) The admin form lands at the exact motivational trough and the live data shows it is being skipped. endSession forces showView('review') with three inputs per execution (actual duration, difficulty 1-5, notes). Of the 8 executions in the DB, 6 have difficultyRating:null, 8 of 8 have notes:"", and 7 of 8 have actualDuration:1. handleFinish then loops native confirm() dialogs, one per task (reviewView.js:83). -> You finish a chore feeling good and are immediately handed a spreadsheet, so you tab through it without reading and the app collects nothing but noise.
- [major] (tasks) Proposed cards are 528px each and 199px of that (38%) is a Locations checkbox fieldset that almost nobody fills. Live: 8 location checkboxes and growing, one per row; exactly 1 of 17 active tasks has any locationIds. buildEnrichmentPrompt (aiEnrich.js:3-13) never asks the AI for locations — it requests only category, estimatedDuration and schedule. -> The tallest control on the approval form is the only one the AI won't pre-fill, so a 10-task brain dump becomes ~5,300px of manual form and pushes 'Active Tasks' to roughly y=5,800.
- [major] (tasks) The library has no sort, filter, search or grouping, and no due signal. renderActive (tasksView.js:214-221) maps getActiveTasks() straight out of listAllTasks' _date_modified-desc order into identical 110px cards. lastCompletedDate is stored on every task record and rendered nowhere. Live: 11 overdue, 0 due today, 6 future, all visually identical, all showing 'Scheduled: 8/6/2026' in 12px grey. -> You cannot tell at a glance what is late, what is coming, or when you last did anything — so the list is scanned, not used, and the overdue work stays overdue.
- [major] (tasks) Archived tasks sit permanently at the bottom of the main working screen and nothing can ever be deleted. #archivedCards begins at y=3662 within the default view and currently contains 'QA calendar verification 20260807-090252' and 'QA </li><img src=x onerror="globalThis…'. A grep for freezr.delete across the whole app returns nothing — archiving only sets status:'archived'. -> Test junk and abandoned chores accumulate forever on the screen you open first, and there is no action anywhere in the product that removes them.
- [major] (session) Starting work takes four clicks through a three-step gate, and one of the three primary chips is structurally dead. The path is nav 'Start Session' → time chip (or focus Custom and type) → 'Propose Bundle' → 'Start Doing'; the screen shows nothing but controls until Propose is pressed. buildBundle (bundleLogic.js:11-13) requires estimatedDuration > 0, and the shortest live task is 10 min, so the '5 min' chip returns 'No tasks fit this time budget' every time. Forgetting the time budget fires a native alert() (sessionView.js:60). -> In the 'I have 20 minutes before guests arrive' moment you spend four clicks on ceremony the app didn't need, and a third of the headline options can never work.
- [major] (session) The bundle is take-it-or-leave-it and under-fills the budget. Verified live: a 30-min budget proposed 25 min (Water the plants 10 + Change bed sheets 15) while three separate 10-min tasks existed. prioritizeTasks (bundleLogic.js:1-8) sorts on scheduledDate alone; 11 tasks share 8/6/2026, so ties silently fall through to _date_modified order. currentProposal is never mutable — there is no remove, swap, reorder or add. -> The app hands you a list you cannot argue with, wastes a sixth of the time you offered it, and orders the work by when you last edited a record rather than by anything you'd recognise as priority.
- [major] (doing) Doing is a one-way conveyor with no map and no stakes. state.currentBundleIndex is only ever incremented (doingView.js:178) — no skip, no back, no view of what's left. buildDoingTaskHtml (taskPresentationLogic.js:21-34) renders a count-UP timer that never references task.estimatedDuration or the session budget, and four buttons of identical size and weight where the destructive one (End Session) sits beside the primary one (Done). Nothing marks a completion — the DOM is simply replaced. -> You cannot see how much of the session remains, cannot reorder around what you're actually near in the house, can end the whole thing by mis-tapping the fourth button, and get no acknowledgement for finishing anything.
- [major] (review) The duration heuristic will poison its own estimates. finishTask computes actualDuration as Math.round((endTime - taskStartTime)/60000) || 1 (doingView.js:79), so any sub-30-second interaction is recorded as 1 minute — 7 of the 8 live executions read 1. suggestDuration averages the last 5 and suggestDurationUpdates fires once a task has 3 non-cancelled executions, so it will offer 'Update this task's estimated duration to 1 min?'. -> Accepting the app's own suggestion shrinks estimatedDuration toward 1, after which buildBundle will cram absurd bundles into a small budget and the time estimates stop meaning anything.
- [major] (history) History is indexed by session, so it cannot answer 'did I already do this?'. buildHistory (historyLogic.js:7-18) groups executions under sessions; the only interaction is one click per accordion row. There is no search, no per-task occurrence list, no totals, streaks or trends, and no way to resume or clean up the orphaned status:'active' session it renders as 'abandoned'. -> Answering 'when did I last descale the coffee machine?' means expanding session rows one by one and reading names — unbounded work that grows with every session you complete.
- [minor] (doing) The one generous moment in the whole product is a native OS dialog. Finishing ≥3 min early fires confirm('You finished early - add "X" (5 min)?') at doingView.js:219, and the same pattern covers the pending-update discard (doingView.js:229), the archive confirmation (categoryLocationView.js:199) and the duration suggestion loop (reviewView.js:83). -> A moment that should feel like a small win arrives as unstyled browser chrome that blocks the page, cannot be animated, and reads as an error.

Recommendations:
- (medium) Split 'Tasks' into three routed screens with distinct jobs: Today (home — overdue and due-today only, each card one tap from 'do this now'), Library (the full task list with sort/filter/search/group-by-category, archive behind a filter chip rather than a stacked section), and Inbox (the triage queue, reachable only when non-empty). Move the Categories & Locations manager out of the daily path entirely into a Settings route. — Measured live, the daily-use surface starts at y=1542 inside a 3,996px scroll, buried under a weekly-use capture box and a usually-empty triage queue. Each of those four regions serves a different rhythm — daily, weekly, on-demand, never — and they should not share a scroll position.
- (medium) Add hash routing (#/today, #/library, #/inbox, #/task/:id, #/session, #/doing/:taskId, #/review/:sessionId, #/history) and session resume. On boot, query sessions for status:'active' and offer Resume / Discard. Mirror bundle, index and per-task elapsed time into localStorage keyed by the session _id, with the DB as source of truth. — No routing or persistence exists anywhere in the codebase, and the live DB already holds an orphaned active session — the data loss is not hypothetical. Routes also make every other recommendation here addressable and shareable.
- (medium) Collapse the session gate into one screen with a live, editable preview. Time chips (add 20, remember the last custom value) recompute the bundle instantly — delete the Propose button. Render the bundle as editable chips: remove refills the gap via findFillerTask (already written and unused for this), '+ what else fits' pulls from the remainder, drag to reorder. One Start button. Grey out or hide a chip whose budget yields nothing instead of letting it fail. — Four clicks of ceremony stand between 'I have 20 minutes' and the first chore, the screen is blank until you press Propose, and the 5-min chip can never succeed with this task set. Everything needed to render a bundle exists the moment a time is chosen.
- (medium) Separate capture from approval and make approval one gesture. 'Add' saves and closes, full stop. Enrichment fires automatically on save and streams into the Inbox cards via freezr.llm.ask's streamBack/onDelta. Each Inbox card shows the AI's guess as one readable sentence ('Clean / Reset · 15 min · every 2 weeks · starting Sat 9 Aug') with a visible AI marker, and a single large Approve button; tapping any chip opens just that one control. Extend buildEnrichmentPrompt to propose scheduledDate and locations too. — Romain already named enrichment as the direction that works. Today it is a second manual button, gated to unenriched proposed tasks, non-streaming, and forbidden from suggesting the one field that blocks approval — so the brain-dump moment ends in ~5 decisions × N tasks of stacked 528px forms.
- (small) Demote Locations out of the approval form — replace the 199px checkbox fieldset with a compact chip row, or drop it from approval entirely and expose it only in task detail. — 199px of every 528px approval card (38%) goes to a field used by 1 of 17 active tasks, and the list grows one row per location added (already 8). It is the worst area-per-value ratio in the app and it is what makes the brain-dump moment feel endless.
- (small) Derive a due state (overdue / today / soon / later) from scheduledDate and let it drive colour, ordering and a count badge on Today. Surface lastCompletedDate on every card as 'last done 12 days ago'. — 11 of 17 tasks are currently overdue and render identically to one due in 2027; lastCompletedDate is already stored on every record and displayed nowhere. This is the cheapest change with the largest effect on whether the list gets used or scanned.
- (medium) Rebuild Doing around the bundle rather than an index. Show every task in the session as a checklist, tap any to work it, complete in any order, tap to skip or defer. Replace the count-up timer with a countdown ring against estimatedDuration sitting inside a session-budget bar. Make Done the single primary action, Already Done / Skip secondary, End Session a small text link. Add a brief confetti or check animation on completion, and route #/doing/:taskId so a reload lands back on the same chore. — currentBundleIndex only increments, the two numbers that make a session a session (task target, budget remaining) are invisible, and the destructive button is the same size as the primary one. This is exactly the 'playful, focused, positive feedback' experience the issue tracker already committed to.
- (medium) Delete the Review screen. Move difficulty to a one-tap emoji row (😌🙂😐😖😫) on the completion card in Doing, dismissible, alongside an optional 'adjust time' chip. Turn the end-of-session screen into a reward: what you did, minutes reclaimed, streak — read-only, no inputs. Notes become an optional action inside task detail. — The live data shows the form is being skipped (6/8 ratings null, 8/8 notes empty), because it lands precisely when motivation to do admin is lowest. Rating in the moment costs one tap and captures the signal while it's fresh; the session end should pay you rather than bill you.
- (medium) Add a task detail route (#/task/:id) showing name, schedule, last done, next due, the full execution history with durations/ratings/notes, plus edit and archive. Make it the shared target of Today cards, Library rows and History entries. — This is the screen that answers 'did I already do this?', which today requires expanding session accordions one at a time. It is also already-agreed direction in the issue tracker, and it gives the inline edit form somewhere to live instead of expanding inside a list card.
- (small) Replace all five native alert/confirm calls with in-app UI: the filler offer becomes an inline '+ Add Water the plants (10 min)' card in the Doing checklist, the duration suggestion becomes a passive dismissible banner on the task rather than a modal loop, and the missing-time-budget alert disappears once the session preview is live. — Three of the five sit inside the session and review flow — the part that is supposed to feel playful. Unstyled OS chrome that blocks the page reads as an error, and a confirm() loop over N tasks at the end of Review is the single most punishing interaction in the app.
- (small) Fix the duration signal before it feeds back: record actualDuration at second granularity (or at minimum drop the `|| 1` floor), require three executions of at least a real minute before suggestDuration fires, and surface the result as a dismissible suggestion on the task card rather than a confirm loop. — Math.round(ms/60000) || 1 records every sub-30-second interaction as 1 minute — 7 of 8 live executions read 1 — so the heuristic will converge on suggesting 1-minute estimates, after which buildBundle packs nonsense into every budget. The learning loop is currently a corruption loop.
- (medium) Add a real Delete (freezr.delete) alongside Archive, and give scheduled work a time surface: an agenda strip of the next 14 days grouped by date on Today, and a month view for seasonal jobs. — There is no delete call anywhere in the app, so QA junk like 'QA calendar verification 20260807-090252' is permanent on the main screen. And the only time affordance today is a 12px grey toLocaleDateString(), which cannot serve the weekly or seasonal maintenance rhythm at all.

## Lens: heuristics
Headline: Chore Planner faithfully renders its data model and almost none of its user's intent: 11 of its 17 tasks are overdue right now and look pixel-identical to one due in October, while the app's only irreversible action — Archive, which has no restore and no delete anywhere in the codebase — is also the one it performs silently on your behalf when you finish a chore.

Findings:
- [blocker] (doing) scheduleLogic.js normalizeTaskSchedule (lines 230-233) falls back to {type:'one_off'} for any task lacking both a `schedule` object and a legacy `recurrence` number. taskUpdateForOutcome (lines 109-112) then returns {status:'archived'} for one_off tasks on completion. Live record 'Declutter my desk' renders in the app right now as 'Organize · 30 min · Once' — verified in the browser — and completing it in a session will permanently archive it. No message, no undo. -> Doing the chore destroys the chore, and the user only finds out when it silently vanishes from Active Tasks into the graveyard at the bottom of the page.
- [blocker] (tasks) handleActiveClick (tasksView.js:372-380) writes status:'archived' on the click — no confirm, no undo, no busy state, and no try/catch, unlike every neighbouring path which routes through saveTaskWithRefresh. archivedTaskCardHtml (tasksView.js:280-285) renders zero buttons — verified in the browser: 0 buttons in #archivedCards. `grep freezr.delete` returns nothing app-wide. Meanwhile archiving a *category* does pop a confirm (categoryLocationView.js:199) and archived categories get a Restore button (line 147). -> One misplaced tap removes a task forever, a failed write leaves no trace at all, and the app protects its taxonomy more carefully than the content the user actually typed.
- [blocker] (tasks) buildActiveTaskDetailsHtml (taskPresentationLogic.js:77-81) prints 'Scheduled: <date>' as the third of three identical 12px #666 lines. Nothing anywhere in the presentation layer compares a scheduledDate to today. Verified live: 11 of 17 cards read 'Scheduled: 8/6/2026' (yesterday) and are visually indistinguishable from the card reading '10/5/2026'. -> The one fact the user opens a chore app to learn — what is due — is the fact the app knows and refuses to say.
- [major] (session) The only offered budgets are 5/15/30 min chips, and buildBundle (bundleLogic.js:18-23) is greedy first-fit that admits a task only if its whole estimatedDuration fits the remaining budget. Live data: 6 of 17 active tasks exceed 30 min (120, 90, 60, 60, 60, 45) — 435 of 615 total backlog minutes. The single most overdue task, 'Round of small fixes around the house' (2h, due 8/6), can never appear in a bundle unless the user notices the unlabelled bare `Custom:` number input beside the chips. -> 71% of the user's workload by time is invisible to the app's central feature, and nothing on screen explains why those tasks never get proposed.
- [major] (tasks) Measured at 390px: document 4406px, #view-tasks 4242px = 5.2 phone screens. The 'Active Tasks' heading begins at y=1542; 'Archived' at y=3870 with 436px of permanent QA junk ('QA calendar verification 20260807-090252', an XSS test string). Reference management, capture, triage, the live backlog and the graveyard are one scroll. renderActive (tasksView.js:214-221) maps getActiveTasks() in _date_modified desc order — so completing a task reshuffles it to the top. No sort, filter, search, grouping, or due/not-due split. -> To see what you have to do you scroll past two screens of forms, then read 17 identical cards in an order that churns every time you touch anything — live duplicates like 'Change bed sheets' ×2 and 'Pay bills' / 'Pay bills, once per month' sit far apart and are undetectable.
- [major] (tasks) Measured live: one proposed-task card is 528px tall (63% of the phone viewport) and contains 22 form controls — category select, 4 location checkboxes, duration, date, repeat select, cadence number, unit select, pattern select, 7 weekday checkboxes, month-day, annual-month, annual-day — before a single 'Approve' button (proposedCardHtml, tasksView.js:168-196). There is no Approve-all, no bulk action, no per-field commit, and no keyboard path. -> Capturing five chores in ten seconds buys you five 528px forms to hand-visit, which is exactly the boring work the user said the AI was supposed to remove.
- [major] (tasks) buildProposedTaskEditorModel (categoryLocationLogic.js:211-220) resolves suggestedCategory straight into the select's `selected` attribute; proposedCardHtml uses `task.suggestedDuration || task.estimatedDuration` for the number field; buildScheduleEditorModel(task, true) seeds the whole schedule editor from suggestedSchedule. Nothing is labelled as a guess. buildApprovedTaskFields (tasksView.js:319-330) then nulls suggestedCategory, suggestedDuration and suggestedSchedule on approve. handleEnrich (tasksView.js:135) also filters `!t.suggestedCategory`, so a badly-guessed task can never be re-enriched and there is no way to clear a suggestion. -> You cannot tell what the machine decided versus what you decided, you cannot revert a single field, and after approval the provenance is gone forever — which matters because the Review screen later offers to overwrite that same duration with measured data.
- [major] (tasks) refreshTasksView (tasksView.js:42-45) refetches the whole tasks collection and re-renders all three lists via innerHTML. Draft preservation exists — captureTaskEditorDrafts / restoreTaskEditorDrafts, lines 60-110 — but is wired only to the categoryLocationStore subscription (line 38), never to refreshTasksView. So approving one proposed card, or pressing Add, wipes every half-filled sibling card. handleAddTasks (lines 116-125) runs one sequential createTask round trip per pasted line with no spinner, no disabled button, and no confirmation of how many were added. -> Triaging a batch is a race against your own saves — finish one card and the rest reset to server state — and pasting eight lines is a silent multi-second freeze with a live button you can double-fire.
- [major] (doing) buildDoingTaskHtml (taskPresentationLogic.js:21-33) renders a 48px counter that counts UP from 00:00, with the target relegated to a 12px grey line ('Clean / Reset · target 10 min'). No countdown, no ring, no overrun state, and no session-level elapsed/remaining anywhere — though sessionView captured timeBudgetMinutes and findFillerTask internally reasons about leftover minutes. renderCurrentTask (doingView.js:66-70) also never clears timerInterval when you leave via the nav, so the clock keeps billing while you browse the Tasks tab. -> On a screen whose entire premise is a time budget, the biggest element is the least informative — and a two-minute detour is silently recorded as chore time that then feeds suggestDuration and permanently inflates the estimate.
- [major] (doing) Done / Already Done / Cancel / End Session are four buttons of identical size, weight, border and colour in a centred row (.doing-actions, index.css:343-348). index.css has no primary, secondary or destructive button style at all — across 439 lines the only non-grey values are #176b2c, #a12121 and #8a5a00, used for status text. 'Cancel' does not back out of anything: it writes a `cancelled` execution and skips the chore, and it sits one thumb-width from 'End Session'. -> The goal action, the give-up action and the quit-everything action are indistinguishable at a glance and one thumb-slip apart, on the screen where the user is standing up holding a mop.
- [major] (doing) maybeAddFillerTask (doingView.js:213-222) fires confirm('You finished early - add "X" (5 min)?'). Behind that click, continueAfterCompletion first awaits a full refreshTasksView() — a whole-collection refetch — so the OS dialog appears after an unindicated network pause. It offers exactly one candidate, no alternative, no 'not now', and the filler is appended to the END of state.currentBundle though the copy implies 'next'. Nothing else acknowledges the completion: the view simply re-renders and the timer resets to 00:00. -> The single moment worth celebrating is answered by a system dialog asking you to do more work, and finishing a chore looks exactly like a click that didn't register.
- [major] (review) suggestDurationUpdates (reviewView.js:76-86) loops tasks and calls confirm("Update this task's estimated duration to 12 min based on recent executions?") — 'this task' is never named, the current value is never shown, and a five-task session produces up to five sequential blind dialogs. Each iteration also runs listExecutionsByTask, a full taskExecutions query. handleFinish (line 61) has no busy state and no double-click guard. If you navigate away instead of pressing Finish, everything typed is lost — it lives only in executionsCache (line 9) — and setNavVisible('review', false) never runs, so the Review tab lingers forever. -> You are asked to approve permanent changes to tasks you cannot identify, and the only way to save your ratings is a button that gives no sign it is working.
- [major] (global) There is no localStorage, sessionStorage, location.hash, pushState or beforeunload anywhere in the app (verified by grep). state.js is a plain in-memory object and index.js always ends on showView('tasks'). Reload mid-session and the bundle is gone, the sessions record stays status:'active' forever, and historyLogic.summariseSession stamps it `abandoned: session.status !== 'completed'`. Live: 1 of 8 sessions is stuck 'active' wearing a permanent grey 'abandoned' tag. handleStart (sessionView.js:85-100) has no guard — starting a second session just overwrites state.currentSession and orphans the first. -> A refresh or an accidental Back destroys the session in progress, and the app then records that loss in History as if the user had given up.
- [major] (global) The words name the system, not the intent: 'Add Tasks', 'Needs Review', 'Active Tasks', 'Archived', 'Propose Bundle', 'Start Doing', 'Session Review', 'Retry completion'. Error copy is raw exception text — 'AI enrichment unavailable: AI enrichment returned an invalid response' (tasksView.js:155), 'Could not record completion: <err.message>' (completionSaveLogic.js:12). 'Nothing to enrich' appears while proposed cards are visibly on screen. 'Add a category before using AI enrichment.' is parked permanently under a disabled button by syncEnrichmentAvailability on every render. The confirm at doingView.js:229 puts a data-consequential choice — 'the schedule was not updated and will need manual correction' — into an OS modal whose OK/Cancel do not map to the question. A fresh install shows three stacked italic denials: 'No tasks awaiting review.' / 'No active tasks.' / 'No archived tasks.' -> Every message describes what the code just did rather than what the user should do next, and the first-run experience is three sentences about absence and a textarea.

Recommendations:
- (medium) Split the Tasks page into three destinations — Inbox (capture + triage), Chores (the organized backlog), and Setup (categories & locations, moved out of the main scroll) — and add hash routing so each has a URL that survives reload. Today all four jobs share one 4242px scroll and index.js always lands on 'tasks'. — The user arrives with one of three unrelated intents (dump a thought, sort out what I dumped, see what I have to do) and currently has to scroll past the other two to reach any of them.
- (medium) Make 'due' the organizing principle of the backlog: group cards into Overdue / Today / This week / Later, each with a count and a total-minutes figure, sorted by scheduledDate rather than _date_modified. Style overdue distinctly. Move Archived off the main screen behind a link, and give archived cards Restore and Delete buttons. — 11 of 17 live tasks are overdue and nothing says so; the list order currently churns whenever you touch anything; and 436px of QA junk is permanently pinned to the bottom of the primary screen with no way to remove it.
- (medium) Replace the count-up timer with a countdown against the task's target — a ring or bar that fills, turns amber past target — nested inside a session-level strip showing tasks done, minutes spent and minutes left of the budget. Clear timerInterval when the doing view is hidden and record only foreground time. — The screen's premise is a time budget and its largest element currently conveys none of it; the running interval also inflates recorded durations, which then permanently corrupt the estimate via suggestDuration.
- (small) Introduce a real button vocabulary in index.css (primary / secondary / quiet / destructive — none exist today) and rebuild the doing action row around it: one full-width primary 'Done', a secondary row for 'Already done' and 'Skip for now' (renamed from Cancel), and 'End session' demoted to a header affordance away from the thumb zone. — Four identical buttons where one is the goal, one is a give-up and one quits everything is the highest-consequence ambiguity in the app, and there is currently no styling language available to fix it.
- (medium) Retire all five native alert/confirm calls for in-page UI: preselect 15 min and disable Propose with an inline hint instead of alert() (sessionView.js:60); turn the filler offer into a dismissible card shown AFTER the completion animation with two or three candidates and a 'not now' (doingView.js:219); turn the duration suggestions into named rows on the Review screen — 'Change bed sheets: 10 → 12 min [Update] [Keep]' — applied on Finish rather than five blind sequential dialogs (reviewView.js:83); turn the pending-schedule warning into an inline banner with 'Retry' and 'End anyway' buttons (doingView.js:229); keep the category-archive confirm but give it the same in-page treatment. — Every one of these is a real decision delivered in an unstyleable modal that steals focus, cannot show the task name or the current value, and offers OK/Cancel where the question needs two verbs.
- (medium) Make the AI's answers visibly the AI's: render suggested category/duration/schedule in a distinct 'suggested' state with a per-field revert, keep suggestedCategory/suggestedDuration/suggestedSchedule on the record after approval instead of nulling them, add 'Approve all suggestions', and allow re-enriching a task that already has a guess. Stream the batch with streamBack/onDelta so cards fill in one at a time instead of one spinner for the whole run. — This is the feature the owner already likes; today its output is silently indistinguishable from the user's own input, unrevertable, unrepeatable, and erased on approve.
- (medium) Collapse the proposed-task card to a one-line summary — name, suggested category chip, duration, schedule phrase — that is approvable with a single tap, with the 22-control editor behind a 'Change' disclosure. Support keyboard triage (Enter approves, Tab moves to the next card). — A 528px form per captured thought means capture is cheap and triage is expensive, which is backwards; the default state should already be good enough to accept.

Keep the full task list one tap away for management. — Activation energy is the entire problem: the decision to start is the hardest step, and every control between opening the app and seeing concrete work is a place to quit. Showing the plan first inverts the choice from 'do I want to start?' (abstract, aversive) to 'do I want to do this specific 12-minute thing?' (concrete, evaluable).
- (small) Rank by slippage ratio, not date. Replace prioritizeTasks' scheduledDate.localeCompare with (daysOverdue / cadenceDays) descending, so 'Water the plants' (every 3d, 1 day late = 33% slipped) outranks 'Plan Christmas vacations' (every 365d, 1 day late = 0.3%). Then stop packing the budget to the brim — fill to roughly 80% and show the slack explicitly ('12 of 15 min — room to breathe'). — Today the model has one dimension and treats an annual car appointment and a wilting plant as equally urgent because both are dated 8/06. Relative slippage is what 'overdue' actually means in a house. The slack is planning-fallacy insurance: a bundle that finishes early produces a win, a bundle sized to exactly fill the budget produces an overrun every single time.
- (medium) Put the reward at the instant of the tap, on the card itself, and make it information rather than praise. On Done: the card fills and collapses in ~250ms, the session ring closes one segment, and the honest fact lands in its place — 'Clean the WCs · next due 21 Aug · 4th time since June'. No adjectives, no exclamation marks. Reserve any celebratory motion (the confetti the tracker asks for) for session completion and genuine milestones only, never per task, and never with congratulatory copy. — Immediate contingent reinforcement is the whole mechanism, and it must be earned to survive a developer's scepticism: a fact he did not previously have reads as competence, while 'Great job!' reads as a product manager pretending to care. Per-task confetti dies to hedonic adaptation in three days and then reads as noise; rarity is what protects it.

- (medium) Build the house-state view on data already stored. Render lastCompletedDate on every task card as 'last done 12 days ago · every 7', and add a freshness view grouped by area: a short bar per room showing time-since-done over cadence, plus one honest headline — 'nothing has slipped more than a week' or '3 things have slipped past double their cadence'. Freshness is the honest metric because it is self-correcting: it degrades gracefully, it is always recoverable in one session, and it maps to something the user can actually perceive by walking around.
